The Challenge

Problem Statement

Unemployment persists in Rwanda's informal sector, particularly affecting the youth in both urban and rural areas. According to the National Institute of Statistics of Rwanda, the youth unemployment rate in 2025 ranges between 15.4% and 21%. Limited access to employment opportunities and low productivity contribute to continuous underemployment and economic vulnerability. Addressing this issue is critical to promoting inclusive economic growth by 2030.

Our Answer

Our Solution

We are building a digital job platform that connects Rwanda's youth with flexible, project-based digital work opportunities in the informal sector — from freelancing and graphic design to social media management and data entry. A central hub where young talent meets real demand, backed by trust.

Who It Serves

Youth seeking flexible digital work

Businesses needing affordable digital services

Built by tech students, startups & youth organizations in partnership with companies and government programs

Youth Profiles & Portfolios

Young people create detailed profiles showcasing their skills, experience, and work samples — their digital CV for the informal economy.

Short-Term Job Listings

Businesses post short-term and project-based digital jobs — graphic design, social media, data entry, freelance tasks — at affordable rates.

Ratings & Reviews

A two-way rating and review system builds trust between clients and workers, creating accountability and reputation in the digital informal market.

Key Advantage

Focused on Flexible Digital Work

Unlike general job boards, our platform is built specifically for flexible, digital, informal work — designed around how Rwanda's youth actually work and earn.

Digital Opportunities on the Platform

Freelancing Graphic Design Social Media Management Data Entry + More digital services

How the Platform Works

01

Youth Signs Up

Create a profile, list skills, upload portfolio samples, and set availability for project-based work.

02

Business Posts a Job

Companies post short-term digital tasks with a brief, budget, and timeline. Simple and fast.

03

Match & Deliver

Youth apply or get matched to relevant opportunities and complete the work on agreed terms.

04

Rate & Grow

Both parties leave reviews. Strong ratings build reputation, opening doors to more and better opportunities.
